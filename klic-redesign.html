About

Built by MKCL, trusted nationwide.

Maharashtra Knowledge Corporation Limited (MKCL) was promoted by the Department of Higher and Technical Education, Government of Maharashtra, and incorporated under the Companies Act, 1956.

Since 2018, MKCL has operated under the General Administration Department, bringing knowledge-led learning to learners across India.
